What the book teaches

  • Choose the right chart for comparisons, trends, and proportions.
  • Choose diagrams, timelines, flowcharts, and roadmaps for non-numerical information.
  • Understand what to visualize before opening design software.
  • Use the companion Visual Library, Visual Finder, and Visual Builder to explore visual formats.

Example: bar charts help compare category values; timelines show sequences of events; flowcharts explain processes.
